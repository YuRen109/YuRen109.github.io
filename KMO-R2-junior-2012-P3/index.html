What We Learned

  2. For exponential Diophantine equations, factoring a difference of powers can turn the original equation into a problem about the distribution of prime factors among several integer factors.

  3. Congruences modulo small integers can determine which prime powers are allowed to divide each factor, greatly reducing the possible forms of the variables.
