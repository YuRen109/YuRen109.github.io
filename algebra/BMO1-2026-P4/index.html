  3. Examine the relative orderings of the first two terms of the three sequences.
  4. In the remaining case, consider the first index at which the alternating ordering pattern fails.

What We Learned

  1. Claim 1 illustrates how the relative order of two recursively defined sequences can be determined by their relative order at two consecutive indices.

  2. In recursively defined sequences, a condition involving only a finite number of consecutive terms may impose restrictions on infinitely many subsequent terms.
    In this problem, once a particular relative order is established at two consecutive indices, the recurrence relation ensures that this order is preserved thereafter.
