What We Learned

  1. A functional inequality can be converted into sign information.
    Instead of trying to determine the values of explicitly, it can be more effective to construct an auxiliary expression whose sign is controlled for every pair of inputs.

  2. Strictness can provide the final contradiction.
    A non-strict inequality may only force an auxiliary quantity to be nonnegative, while the existence of one strict case can strengthen this to strict positivity at a particular pair. This distinction can rule out an otherwise possible equality case.
